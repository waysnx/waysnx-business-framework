# WBF-DOC-0124 – Technology Selection Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines the Technology Selection methodology within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes a standardized approach for evaluating, selecting, approving, governing, and managing technologies used across the enterprise.

The guide ensures that technology decisions are driven by business value, architectural alignment, operational excellence, security, scalability, maintainability, and long-term sustainability rather than personal preference or short-term convenience.

It provides a repeatable evaluation framework for programming languages, frameworks, databases, cloud services, AI platforms, infrastructure technologies, security products, development tools, and third-party software.

This guide applies to enterprise architects, solution architects, engineering managers, technical leads, developers, DevOps engineers, security teams, procurement teams, and technology governance boards.

# 2. Definitions

### Technology Selection

A structured process for identifying, evaluating, comparing, approving, and governing technologies that support enterprise business objectives.

---

### Technology Standard

An approved technology that is recommended for enterprise-wide adoption.

---

### Technology Exception

An approved deviation from enterprise technology standards based on documented business or technical justification.

---

### Technology Lifecycle

The managed lifecycle of a technology from evaluation through retirement.

---

### Proof of Concept (PoC)

A limited implementation used to validate technical feasibility, business value, performance, integration, and operational suitability before enterprise adoption.

---

# 3. Objectives

The Technology Selection Guide should:

- Standardize technology evaluation
- Align technology with business strategy
- Promote architectural consistency
- Encourage technology reuse
- Reduce operational risk
- Improve security and compliance
- Prevent unnecessary technology proliferation
- Optimize technology investments
- Support innovation
- Enable continuous technology modernization

# 4. Technology Selection Principles

Technology selection should be:

- Business Driven
- Architecture Aligned
- Vendor Neutral
- Standards Based
- Secure by Design
- Cloud Ready
- AI Ready
- Operationally Mature
- Cost Effective
- Future Ready

Technology decisions should balance innovation with long-term maintainability and operational sustainability.

# 5. Technology Evaluation Lifecycle

## Phase 1 – Business Need

Activities:

- Define business objectives
- Identify technical requirements
- Identify constraints
- Define success criteria

Deliverables:

- Technology Request
- Business Justification

---

## Phase 2 – Market Research

Activities:

- Identify candidate technologies
- Compare alternatives
- Review industry adoption
- Review community maturity

Deliverables:

- Technology Shortlist
- Comparative Analysis

---

## Phase 3 – Technical Evaluation

Activities:

- Evaluate architecture fit
- Assess scalability
- Assess performance
- Evaluate integration capabilities
- Review maintainability

Deliverables:

- Technical Assessment
- Evaluation Matrix

---

## Phase 4 – Security & Compliance Assessment

Activities:

- Security review
- Privacy review
- License validation
- Compliance assessment
- Supply chain risk analysis

Deliverables:

- Security Assessment
- Compliance Report

---

## Phase 5 – Proof of Concept

Activities:

- Implement pilot
- Validate integrations
- Measure performance
- Evaluate developer experience
- Validate operational readiness

Deliverables:

- PoC Report
- Benchmark Results

---

## Phase 6 – Technology Approval

Activities:

- Architecture Review Board evaluation
- Risk assessment
- Executive approval
- Technology classification

Deliverables:

- Approval Decision
- Technology Standard

---

## Phase 7 – Adoption & Governance

Activities:

- Publish standards
- Update repositories
- Provide implementation guidance
- Monitor technology adoption

Deliverables:

- Technology Standard
- Implementation Guide
- Adoption Metrics

---

## Phase 8 – Lifecycle Management

Activities:

- Monitor maturity
- Assess upgrades
- Plan migration
- Manage retirement

Deliverables:

- Technology Roadmap
- Retirement Plan

# 10. Best Practices

- Define business requirements before evaluating technologies.
- Use objective evaluation criteria.
- Validate through Proof of Concept.
- Prefer enterprise standards.
- Evaluate total cost of ownership.
- Consider long-term support and community maturity.
- Review licensing and legal implications.
- Assess operational readiness.
- Review technologies periodically.

---

# 11. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Technology selection based on personal preference
- Adopting immature technologies without validation
- Ignoring enterprise standards
- Duplicate technology stacks
- Vendor lock-in without assessment
- Missing Proof of Concept
- Ignoring security reviews
- Underestimating operational costs
- Failing to retire obsolete technologies
