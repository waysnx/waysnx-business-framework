# WBF-DOC-0123 – Architecture Review Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines the Architecture Review process within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes a standardized approach for evaluating architecture quality, ensuring alignment with enterprise standards, identifying risks, validating architectural decisions, and governing solution implementation before development and deployment.

Architecture reviews provide assurance that business solutions conform to enterprise architecture principles, technology standards, security requirements, cloud strategies, data governance, AI governance, and operational best practices.

This guide applies to enterprise architects, solution architects, technical architects, engineering managers, development teams, security teams, governance boards, and executive stakeholders.

# 2. Definitions

### Architecture Review

A structured evaluation of an architectural design to determine its alignment with enterprise standards, business objectives, governance requirements, and quality expectations.

---

### Architecture Review Board (ARB)

A governance body responsible for reviewing, approving, rejecting, or requesting changes to architectural designs.

---

### Review Findings

Documented observations, risks, recommendations, issues, and improvement opportunities identified during architecture reviews.

---

### Architecture Compliance

The degree to which a solution conforms to enterprise principles, standards, reference models, and governance policies.

---

### Architecture Exception

An approved deviation from enterprise architecture standards that includes documented justification, risk assessment, and mitigation plan.

# 4. Architecture Review Principles

Architecture reviews should be:

- Business Focused
- Independent
- Objective
- Collaborative
- Risk Based
- Standards Driven
- Evidence Based
- Constructive
- Transparent
- Continuous

The purpose of a review is to improve solution quality—not simply to approve or reject designs.

# 5. Architecture Review Lifecycle

## Phase 1 – Review Initiation

Activities

- Identify review scope
- Assign reviewers
- Collect architecture artifacts
- Schedule review

Deliverables

- Review Charter
- Review Plan

---

## Phase 2 – Documentation Review

Activities

- Review business requirements
- Review architecture diagrams
- Review ADRs
- Review standards compliance

Deliverables

- Documentation Assessment

---

## Phase 3 – Technical Assessment

Activities

- Validate architecture
- Review integrations
- Review technology selections
- Evaluate scalability

Deliverables

- Technical Assessment Report

---

## Phase 4 – Security & Compliance Review

Activities

- Threat assessment
- Privacy review
- Security controls review
- Regulatory compliance validation

Deliverables

- Security Assessment
- Compliance Report

---

## Phase 5 – Operational Review

Activities

- Review deployment strategy
- Review monitoring
- Review disaster recovery
- Review operational readiness

Deliverables

- Operations Readiness Report

---

## Phase 6 – Review Decision

Possible outcomes

- Approved
- Approved with Conditions
- Rework Required
- Rejected

Deliverables

- Architecture Decision
- Action Items

---

## Phase 7 – Follow-up Review

Activities

- Validate corrections
- Close findings
- Update repository
- Capture lessons learned

Deliverables

- Final Review Report
- Lessons Learned

# 10. Best Practices

- Conduct reviews early.
- Review business value before technology.
- Use standardized checklists.
- Include cross-functional reviewers.
- Record all architectural decisions.
- Focus on measurable risks.
- Validate non-functional requirements.
- Track review actions to closure.
- Continuously improve review criteria.

---

# 11. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Rubber-stamp approvals
- Technology-only reviews
- Late-stage reviews
- Missing documentation
- Ignoring security
- Subjective decision making
- Untracked action items
- Informal exception approvals
- Architecture reviews without governance
