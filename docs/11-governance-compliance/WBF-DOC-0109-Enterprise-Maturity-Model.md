# WBF-DOC-0109 – Enterprise Maturity Model

## Purpose

This specification defines the Enterprise Maturity Model within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the principles, governance model, maturity levels, assessment methodology, enterprise capabilities, and continuous improvement framework used to evaluate and improve organizational capabilities across business, technology, governance, operations, security, quality, data, artificial intelligence, and enterprise architecture.

The Enterprise Maturity Model provides a standardized approach for measuring organizational capability, identifying improvement opportunities, prioritizing investments, benchmarking performance, and guiding enterprise transformation initiatives.

This specification is technology independent and applies across all business units, enterprise architecture domains, digital products, operational services, governance functions, engineering organizations, and supporting enterprise capabilities.

# 2. Definitions

### Enterprise Maturity

The measurable capability of an organization to consistently achieve desired business outcomes through standardized, governed, optimized, and continuously improving practices.

### Maturity Assessment

A structured evaluation used to determine capability levels across one or more enterprise domains.

### Capability

A business or technical function that enables the organization to achieve strategic objectives.

### Maturity Indicator

A measurable criterion used to determine organizational capability.

### Improvement Roadmap

A prioritized plan defining actions required to improve organizational maturity.

# 8. Maturity Levels

## Level 1 – Initial

- Ad hoc processes
- Limited governance
- Individual effort
- High variability
- Minimal documentation

---

## Level 2 – Managed

- Basic governance established
- Documented processes
- Repeatable execution
- Defined responsibilities
- Initial metrics

---

## Level 3 – Defined

- Enterprise standards adopted
- Organization-wide consistency
- Formal governance
- Documented architecture
- Integrated processes

---

## Level 4 – Quantitatively Managed

- Performance measured
- KPI-driven management
- Predictive analytics
- Risk-informed decisions
- Automated monitoring

---

## Level 5 – Optimized

- Continuous innovation
- AI-assisted optimization
- Autonomous governance support
- Continuous improvement
- Enterprise-wide optimization

# 12. Best Practices

- Use objective assessment criteria.
- Collect measurable evidence.
- Assess regularly.
- Prioritize high-value improvements.
- Standardize scoring.
- Benchmark across business units.
- Maintain executive visibility.
- Measure improvement over time.
- Integrate maturity into strategic planning.

---

# 13. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Subjective scoring
- One-time assessments
- Missing evidence
- Ignoring improvement plans
- Measuring only documentation
- Comparing unrelated capabilities
- Lack of executive sponsorship
- Assessment without follow-up
- Treating maturity as a compliance exercise
