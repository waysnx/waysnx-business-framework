# WBF-DOC-0106 – Regulatory Compliance

## Purpose

This specification defines the Regulatory Compliance Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the governance model, principles, enterprise capabilities, architectural framework, and lifecycle required to identify, interpret, implement, monitor, assess, and continuously maintain compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and industry obligations.

Regulatory Compliance Architecture enables organizations to systematically integrate regulatory requirements into business operations, enterprise architecture, technology platforms, digital products, operational processes, information management, and governance activities while minimizing legal, financial, operational, and reputational risk.

This specification is technology independent and applies across all business units, enterprise architecture domains, digital products, cloud platforms, AI-enabled systems, operational services, third-party providers, and supporting enterprise functions.

# 2. Definitions

### Regulatory Compliance

The process of ensuring organizational activities comply with applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, industry standards, and governmental requirements.

### Regulatory Requirement

A mandatory obligation imposed by legislation, regulatory authorities, contracts, or recognized industry standards.

### Compliance Obligation

A documented requirement that the organization must satisfy through policies, processes, controls, or operational activities.

### Regulatory Assessment

An evaluation of enterprise activities against applicable regulatory obligations.

### Compliance Evidence

Documented artifacts demonstrating that regulatory obligations have been satisfied.

# 11. Best Practices

- Maintain a centralized regulatory register.
- Continuously monitor regulatory changes.
- Map regulations to enterprise controls.
- Define clear ownership.
- Automate compliance evidence collection.
- Review compliance regularly.
- Integrate compliance into engineering practices.
- Maintain complete audit trails.
- Continuously improve regulatory readiness.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Compliance only before audits
- Unknown regulatory obligations
- Manual regulatory tracking
- Undefined compliance ownership
- Missing evidence
- One-time compliance reviews
- Ignoring regulatory changes
- Compliance managed in silos
- Treating regulations as documentation instead of operational requirements
