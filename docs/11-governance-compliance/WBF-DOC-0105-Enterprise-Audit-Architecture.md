# WBF-DOC-0105 – Enterprise Audit Architecture

## Purpose

This specification defines the Enterprise Audit Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the governance model, principles, architectural framework, lifecycle, and enterprise capabilities required to provide independent assurance regarding governance effectiveness, risk management, internal controls, compliance, operational performance, and enterprise architecture.

Enterprise Audit Architecture enables objective assessment of organizational activities, strengthens accountability, supports executive oversight, improves governance maturity, and promotes continuous organizational improvement through evidence-based evaluations and recommendations.

This specification is technology independent and applies across all business units, enterprise architecture domains, operational functions, digital products, technology platforms, cloud services, AI-enabled systems, programs, projects, and enterprise governance activities.

# 2. Definitions

### Enterprise Audit

An independent and objective evaluation of enterprise activities to determine whether governance, risk management, controls, and operations are functioning effectively.

### Audit Architecture

The enterprise framework defining audit governance, planning, execution, reporting, assurance, and continuous improvement.

### Audit Evidence

Documented information collected to support audit findings and conclusions.

### Audit Finding

A documented observation identifying conformity, nonconformity, improvement opportunity, or organizational risk.

### Audit Recommendation

A proposed action intended to strengthen governance, controls, operational effectiveness, or compliance.

# 11. Best Practices

- Maintain auditor independence.
- Use risk-based audit planning.
- Collect objective evidence.
- Standardize audit methodologies.
- Track corrective actions.
- Monitor recurring findings.
- Measure audit effectiveness.
- Report to executive leadership.
- Continuously improve audit capabilities.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Audits without independence
- Checklist-only audits
- Missing evidence
- Ignoring corrective actions
- Repeating identical findings
- Poor documentation
- One-time audits
- Lack of executive visibility
- Audits focused on blame instead of improvement
