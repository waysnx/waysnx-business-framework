# WBF-DOC-0107 – Business Ethics Architecture

## Purpose

This specification defines the Business Ethics Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the principles, governance model, enterprise capabilities, ethical framework, and organizational responsibilities required to promote integrity, transparency, accountability, fairness, and responsible decision-making throughout the enterprise.

Business Ethics Architecture provides a structured approach for embedding ethical behavior into business strategy, leadership, governance, technology, product development, operational processes, supplier relationships, customer engagement, AI-enabled systems, and organizational culture.

This specification is technology independent and applies across all business units, enterprise architecture domains, operational functions, digital products, technology platforms, cloud services, AI systems, employees, contractors, suppliers, and governance bodies.

# 2. Definitions

### Business Ethics

The principles, values, and standards that guide responsible organizational behavior, decision-making, and professional conduct.

### Ethical Governance

The oversight mechanisms ensuring organizational decisions align with ethical principles and corporate values.

### Code of Ethics

A formal statement describing expected standards of behavior for individuals and the organization.

### Conflict of Interest

A situation where personal interests could improperly influence professional decisions or organizational responsibilities.

### Ethical Decision Making

The structured evaluation of business decisions considering integrity, fairness, accountability, transparency, legal obligations, and societal impact.

# 8. Enterprise Capabilities

Enterprise Business Ethics should support:

## Ethical Governance

Establish enterprise ethical principles, responsibilities, and oversight.

---

## Ethical Decision Support

Provide guidance for ethical decision-making across business and technology.

---

## Ethics Awareness

Educate employees, partners, and leadership regarding organizational values and expected conduct.

---

## Ethics Monitoring

Monitor ethical behavior, policy adherence, conflicts of interest, and organizational culture.

---

## Incident Reporting

Provide secure mechanisms for reporting ethical concerns and misconduct.

---

## Investigation Management

Manage ethical investigations fairly, objectively, confidentially, and consistently.

---

## Ethics Analytics

Analyze trends, cultural indicators, investigations, policy adherence, and organizational maturity.

---

## Continuous Ethical Improvement

Improve ethics programs through governance reviews, feedback, assessments, audits, and organizational learning.

# 11. Best Practices

- Establish a formal Code of Ethics.
- Lead by example.
- Encourage transparent communication.
- Protect whistleblowers.
- Train employees regularly.
- Manage conflicts of interest.
- Embed ethics into AI and technology.
- Measure ethical culture.
- Continuously improve ethical governance.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Ethics only on paper
- Leadership ignoring policies
- Retaliation against whistleblowers
- Hidden conflicts of interest
- Unethical vendor practices
- Ignoring customer concerns
- Biased AI systems
- Lack of accountability
- Culture driven solely by profit
