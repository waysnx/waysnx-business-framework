# WBF-DOC-0102 – Risk Management Architecture

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise Risk Management Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the principles, governance model, architectural framework, lifecycle, and enterprise capabilities required to identify, assess, manage, monitor, and continuously improve risk management across the organization.

Risk Management Architecture provides a consistent and integrated approach to managing strategic, operational, financial, technology, security, compliance, and project risks, enabling informed decision-making, organizational resilience, and sustainable business growth.

This specification is technology independent and applies to all business units, enterprise architecture domains, digital products, technology platforms, operational services, programs, projects, and governance functions.

# 2. Definitions

### Risk

The possibility that an event or condition may negatively or positively impact the achievement of business objectives.

### Risk Management

The coordinated activities used to identify, evaluate, treat, monitor, and communicate enterprise risks.

### Risk Appetite

The amount and type of risk the organization is willing to accept while pursuing its strategic objectives.

### Risk Register

A structured repository containing identified risks, assessments, ownership, treatment plans, and monitoring information.

### Risk Treatment

Actions taken to avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept, or exploit identified risks.

# 8. Enterprise Capabilities

Enterprise Risk Management should support:

## Risk Identification

Identify internal and external risks affecting enterprise objectives.

---

## Risk Assessment

Evaluate probability, impact, exposure, and business consequences.

---

## Risk Treatment

Define and implement mitigation, avoidance, transfer, acceptance, or exploitation strategies.

---

## Risk Monitoring

Continuously monitor enterprise risk indicators, trends, and emerging threats.

---

## Risk Reporting

Provide executive dashboards, governance reports, and operational visibility into enterprise risk posture.

---

## Risk Analytics

Analyze historical trends, risk patterns, and predictive indicators supporting informed decision-making.

---

## Third-Party Risk Management

Assess suppliers, partners, cloud providers, and external service dependencies.

---

## Continuous Risk Improvement

Continuously improve enterprise risk management using reviews, audits, lessons learned, and maturity assessments.

# 11. Best Practices

- Maintain a centralized enterprise risk register.
- Define organizational risk appetite.
- Assign clear risk ownership.
- Review risks regularly.
- Monitor leading risk indicators.
- Integrate risk into strategic planning.
- Assess third-party risks continuously.
- Automate risk reporting where possible.
- Continuously improve risk maturity.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Reactive risk management
- Undefined risk ownership
- Ignoring emerging risks
- Risks without mitigation plans
- Siloed risk registers
- Poor executive visibility
- One-time risk assessments
- Underestimating third-party risk
- Treating compliance as the only risk concern
