# WBF-DOC-0110 – Governance Framework

## Purpose

This specification defines the Enterprise Governance Framework within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the unified governance operating model that integrates governance, policy management, risk management, compliance, regulatory obligations, enterprise audit, business ethics, decision governance, and enterprise maturity into a cohesive enterprise-wide governance system.

The Governance Framework provides the organizational structure, governance principles, lifecycle, capabilities, oversight mechanisms, and continuous improvement processes necessary to ensure effective leadership, accountability, strategic alignment, operational excellence, regulatory compliance, and sustainable enterprise growth.

This specification is technology independent and serves as the authoritative governance framework for all business units, enterprise architecture domains, operational functions, technology platforms, digital products, AI-enabled systems, cloud environments, and supporting enterprise capabilities.

# 2. Definitions

### Enterprise Governance

The system by which an organization is directed, controlled, monitored, and continuously improved to achieve strategic objectives.

### Governance Framework

The integrated model defining governance structures, policies, decision rights, accountability, oversight, controls, assurance mechanisms, and continuous improvement across the enterprise.

### Governance Board

A formal organizational body responsible for oversight, strategic direction, approvals, and governance decisions.

### Governance Control

A policy, process, standard, or mechanism that ensures organizational objectives are achieved while managing risk and compliance.

### Governance Capability

An organizational function enabling effective governance, accountability, transparency, and organizational performance.

# 11. Best Practices

- Define clear governance structures.
- Assign ownership for every governance capability.
- Maintain standardized governance processes.
- Align governance with enterprise strategy.
- Integrate governance with enterprise architecture.
- Measure governance performance.
- Conduct regular governance reviews.
- Encourage transparency and accountability.
- Continuously improve governance maturity.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Fragmented governance structures
- Undefined governance responsibilities
- Excessive bureaucracy
- Duplicate governance processes
- Missing executive sponsorship
- Governance without measurable outcomes
- Weak policy enforcement
- Reactive governance
- Lack of continuous improvement
