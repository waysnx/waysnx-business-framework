# WBF-DOC-0101 – Governance Architecture

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise Governance Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the principles, governance structures, decision-making mechanisms, organizational responsibilities, oversight processes, and accountability model required to direct, control, monitor, and continuously improve enterprise business and technology initiatives.

Governance Architecture provides the enterprise framework for aligning strategic objectives, business capabilities, technology investments, operational activities, risk management, compliance obligations, and architectural decisions while ensuring accountability, transparency, and sustainable organizational growth.

This specification is technology independent and applies to all business units, enterprise architecture domains, operational functions, digital products, technology platforms, projects, programs, portfolios, and organizational governance bodies.

# 2. Definitions

### Governance

The system of policies, decision-making structures, oversight mechanisms, accountability, and controls that direct and monitor enterprise activities toward organizational objectives.

### Governance Architecture

The enterprise structure defining governance domains, organizational responsibilities, decision rights, review processes, policies, and continuous improvement mechanisms.

### Governance Board

A formally established decision-making body responsible for oversight, prioritization, policy approval, risk management, and strategic direction.

### Decision Rights

Clearly defined authority specifying who is responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed for governance decisions.

### Enterprise Oversight

The ongoing monitoring, review, and evaluation of enterprise performance, risks, compliance, and strategic alignment.

# 11. Best Practices

- Establish clear governance structures.
- Define transparent decision rights.
- Standardize enterprise policies.
- Measure governance effectiveness.
- Review governance regularly.
- Align governance with business strategy.
- Encourage evidence-based decisions.
- Integrate governance across business and technology.
- Continuously improve governance maturity.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Governance without accountability
- Undefined ownership
- Excessive bureaucracy
- Reactive governance
- Conflicting policies
- Governance silos
- Missing executive sponsorship
- Poor transparency
- Governance disconnected from business objectives
