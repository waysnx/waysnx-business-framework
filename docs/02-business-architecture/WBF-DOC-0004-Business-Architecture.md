# WBF-DOC-0004 – Business Architecture

## Purpose

Business Architecture defines the structure of the enterprise from a business perspective. It organizes strategy, capabilities, domains, workflows, services, business objects, rules, policies, events, actors, roles, goals and KPIs into a coherent operating model.

## Objectives

- Align business strategy with execution
- Standardize business modeling
- Promote reuse and consistency
- Improve governance and traceability
- Enable technology-independent business design

## Core Building Blocks

Business Strategy
→ Goals
→ Business Capabilities
→ Domains
→ Modules
→ Workflows
→ Business Services
→ Business Objects
→ Business Entities
→ Business Rules
→ Business Policies
→ Business Events
→ Commands / Queries / Decisions
→ Actors / Roles
→ KPIs

## Principles

- Business-first
- Technology independent
- Modular
- Reusable
- Governed
- Traceable

## Governance

Every business artifact should have:
- Unique identifier
- Owner
- Version
- Lifecycle
- Traceability
- Review process

## Deliverables

- Capability Maps
- Domain Models
- Workflow Models
- Service Catalog
- Business Information Model
- Business Rules Catalog
- Policy Register
- Event Catalog
- Goal Register
- KPI Register
