# WBF-DOC-0021 – KPI Specification

## Purpose

A **Key Performance Indicator (KPI)** defines how the achievement of Business Goals, Business Capabilities and operational performance is measured within the WaysNX Business Framework.

## 1. Scope

Defines the identification, measurement, governance and reporting of KPIs across the enterprise.

## 2. Definition

A KPI is a measurable indicator used to evaluate progress toward a defined Business Goal or operational objective.

## 3. Relationship Model

Business Strategy → Goal → KPI → Capability → Workflow → Dashboard

## 4. KPI Categories

- Strategic
- Operational
- Financial
- Customer
- Quality
- Compliance
- Productivity
- Risk

## 5. Principles

- Measurable
- Objective
- Actionable
- Traceable
- Business aligned

## 6. Characteristics

- Defined target
- Defined calculation
- Data source
- Review frequency
- Owner

## 7. KPI Lifecycle

Define → Approve → Measure → Report → Review → Improve → Retire

## 8. Metadata

- KPI ID
- Name
- Description
- Goal
- Formula
- Unit of Measure
- Target Value
- Thresholds
- Frequency
- Data Source
- Owner
- Version
- Status

## 9. Ownership

- Executive Sponsor
- Business Owner
- Process Owner
- Data Steward

## 10. Dependency Rules

- Every KPI should support at least one Goal
- KPI formulas must be documented
- Data sources must be identifiable
- KPI changes require governance approval

## 11. Governance

- KPI catalog
- Review schedule
- Performance reporting
- Audit trail
- Version management

## 12. Deliverables

- KPI Specification
- KPI Register
- Dashboard Mapping
- Measurement Guide

## 13. Best Practices

- Use SMART metrics
- Minimize vanity metrics
- Automate data collection where possible
- Review targets periodically

## 14. Anti-Patterns

- Unmeasurable KPIs
- Conflicting KPIs
- Missing ownership
- Undefined formulas

## 15. Examples

- Recruitment cycle time ≤ 15 days
- Customer satisfaction ≥ 95%
- First-time approval rate ≥ 98%
- Invoice processing time ≤ 2 hours
