## Scope

Defines how goals are identified, managed and measured.

## Definition

A Goal is a measurable target that guides business capabilities and operations.

## Relationship Model

Business Strategy → Goal → Capability → Workflow → KPI

## Goal Categories

- Strategic
- Tactical
- Operational
- Financial
- Customer
- Compliance

## Principles

- Measurable
- Business aligned
- Time-bound
- Traceable
- Owned

## Characteristics

- Success criteria
- Priority
- Target date
- Business owner

## Lifecycle

Identify → Define → Approve → Execute → Measure → Review → Retire

## Metadata

- Goal ID
- Name
- Category
- Owner
- Target Date
- Success Criteria
- Related KPIs
- Version
- Status

## Ownership

Executive Sponsor, Business Owner, Enterprise Architect

## Dependency Rules

- Goals align with strategy
- KPIs measure goals
- Capabilities support goals

## Governance

Goal register, reviews, reporting and change management.

## Deliverables

- Goal Specification
- Goal Register
- KPI Mapping

## Best Practices

- SMART goals
- Clear ownership
- Regular reviews

## Anti-Patterns

- Unmeasurable goals
- No owner
- Conflicting objectives

## Examples

- Reduce onboarding time by 25%
- Increase customer satisfaction to 95%
- Reduce operational cost by 15%
