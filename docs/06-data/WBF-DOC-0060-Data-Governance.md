# WBF-DOC-0060 – Data Governance

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise governance framework, principles, organizational responsibilities, policies, standards, and oversight mechanisms for managing enterprise data within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF).

Data Governance ensures that enterprise information is managed as a strategic organizational asset by establishing clear ownership, accountability, stewardship, quality expectations, compliance requirements, and decision-making processes. It provides the governance foundation that aligns business objectives, regulatory obligations, operational requirements, analytics, and emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence.

This specification is technology independent and applies across all business domains, information assets, and enterprise systems.

# 1. Scope

This specification applies to governance of:

- Master Data
- Transactional Data
- Reference Data
- Metadata
- Analytical Data
- AI & Machine Learning Data
- Enterprise Reports
- Dashboards
- Documents
- APIs
- Integration Platforms
- Cloud Services
- Enterprise Information Assets

It governs enterprise information regardless of technology platform, storage mechanism, or deployment model.

---

# 2. Definitions

### Data Governance

The enterprise framework of policies, roles, responsibilities, standards, and processes that ensure information is managed consistently throughout its lifecycle.

### Data Owner

The business authority accountable for the definition, usage, quality, security, and governance of enterprise information.

### Data Steward

The individual or organizational role responsible for implementing governance policies and maintaining enterprise data quality.

### Data Policy

An enterprise rule defining how information should be created, managed, protected, shared, retained, and disposed.

### Data Standard

A documented enterprise requirement ensuring consistency in information management across business domains.

# 3. Objectives

Data Governance should:

- Treat data as a strategic enterprise asset
- Define clear ownership and accountability
- Improve enterprise consistency
- Support regulatory compliance
- Improve decision-making
- Protect enterprise information
- Enable trusted analytics and AI
- Promote continuous improvement

# 4. Data Governance Principles

Enterprise data governance should be:

- Business Driven
- Enterprise Wide
- Accountable
- Transparent
- Governed
- Secure
- Risk Aware
- Compliance Focused
- Measurable
- Continuously Improved

Governance decisions should be driven by business value while balancing operational efficiency, risk, and compliance.

# 10. Best Practices

- Establish executive sponsorship for data governance.
- Define ownership for every critical data asset.
- Standardize enterprise data policies.
- Assign dedicated data stewards.
- Continuously monitor governance metrics.
- Periodically review governance policies.
- Align governance with enterprise architecture.
- Integrate governance into business processes.
- Promote enterprise-wide data literacy.

---

# 11. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Governance without executive sponsorship
- Undefined ownership
- Inconsistent business definitions
- Governance treated solely as an IT responsibility
- Missing stewardship
- Poor policy enforcement
- Reactive governance
- Ignoring compliance obligations
- Unmeasured governance effectiveness
