# WBF-DOC-0080 – AI Governance

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise AI Governance framework within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the organizational structures, governance principles, policies, decision-making processes, accountability models, and oversight mechanisms required to ensure Artificial Intelligence capabilities are developed, deployed, operated, and continuously improved in a controlled, secure, compliant, and business-aligned manner.

AI Governance provides strategic direction for the entire AI ecosystem by aligning AI initiatives with enterprise objectives while ensuring responsible innovation, regulatory compliance, operational excellence, and sustainable business value.

This specification is technology independent and applies to every AI capability across the enterprise, including AI Agents, Generative AI, Machine Learning, Intelligent Automation, Knowledge Platforms, AI-enabled Applications, and future AI technologies.

# 1. Scope

This specification applies to:

- Enterprise AI Strategy
- AI Programs
- AI Projects
- AI Products
- AI Platforms
- AI Agents
- Machine Learning
- Generative AI
- Intelligent Automation
- AI-enabled Enterprise Applications

It governs all AI initiatives across the enterprise regardless of technology provider or deployment model.

---

# 2. Definitions

### AI Governance

The enterprise discipline responsible for directing, controlling, monitoring, and continuously improving Artificial Intelligence capabilities throughout their lifecycle.

### AI Policy

Enterprise rules defining how AI capabilities may be developed, deployed, and operated.

### AI Portfolio

The collection of AI initiatives, platforms, services, models, agents, and applications managed across the enterprise.

### AI Risk

Business, operational, legal, ethical, security, financial, or regulatory risks associated with AI.

### AI Oversight

The organizational process for monitoring AI performance, compliance, safety, effectiveness, and business alignment.

# 3. Objectives

AI Governance should:

- Align AI initiatives with business strategy
- Establish enterprise-wide AI standards
- Promote responsible AI adoption
- Improve governance consistency
- Protect enterprise information
- Reduce operational risk
- Support regulatory compliance
- Maximize business value
- Enable continuous innovation
- Build organizational trust in AI

# 9. Organizational Roles & Responsibilities

Typical governance responsibilities include:

| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|------------------|
| Executive Leadership | AI strategy, investment, organizational accountability |
| Enterprise Architecture | AI architecture standards and reviews |
| AI Governance Board | Policies, approvals, governance oversight |
| Information Security | Security controls and risk management |
| Legal & Compliance | Regulatory compliance and contractual obligations |
| Data Governance | Enterprise data quality and stewardship |
| Business Owners | Business outcomes and operational ownership |
| Product Teams | AI solution implementation and maintenance |
| Operations | Monitoring, support, and incident response |
| Internal Audit | Independent governance assurance |

Governance responsibilities should be clearly defined, documented, and periodically reviewed.

# 11. Best Practices

- Establish an enterprise AI governance board.
- Maintain a centralized AI portfolio.
- Define enterprise AI standards.
- Review AI initiatives before implementation.
- Continuously monitor AI value and risks.
- Maintain comprehensive documentation.
- Encourage responsible innovation.
- Measure governance effectiveness.
- Continuously evolve governance practices.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Uncontrolled AI adoption
- Shadow AI initiatives
- Undefined ownership
- Missing governance policies
- Ignoring business alignment
- Vendor lock-in
- No executive oversight
- Poor documentation
- Reactive governance
