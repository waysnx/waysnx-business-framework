# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> **Answers to common questions about the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF).**

---

# General Questions

## What is the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF)?

The WaysNX Business Framework (WBF) is a technology-independent engineering framework that helps organizations design, document, govern, review, and deliver high-quality software systems.

Rather than focusing on implementation technologies, WBF provides guidance for:

- Business Analysis
- Requirements Engineering
- Software Architecture
- Documentation Standards
- Engineering Governance
- Quality Reviews
- Project Templates
- Engineering Best Practices

---

## Why was WBF created?

Many software projects struggle because of:

- Unclear requirements
- Inconsistent documentation
- Poor architecture
- Knowledge silos
- Weak governance
- Lack of engineering standards

WBF was created to address these challenges by providing a structured framework that promotes consistency, quality, and long-term maintainability.

---

## Is WBF a software development methodology?

No.

WBF is **not** intended to replace Agile, Scrum, SAFe, Kanban, Waterfall, or other project management methodologies.

Instead, WBF complements these approaches by providing engineering standards, documentation practices, governance, and architectural guidance.

---

## Is WBF tied to a specific technology stack?

No.

One of the core principles of WBF is **technology independence**.

The framework can be applied regardless of programming language, framework, cloud platform, database, or development methodology.

---

## Is WBF open source?

Yes.

The WaysNX Business Framework is intended to be openly available so that individuals and organizations can adopt, improve, and contribute to its continued evolution.

---

# Adoption

## Who should use WBF?

WBF is suitable for:

- Software Development Companies
- Enterprise IT Teams
- Consulting Organizations
- Startups
- Product Companies
- Government Organizations
- Educational Institutions
- Independent Architects
- Engineering Managers
- Technical Leads

---

## Can small teams use WBF?

Yes.

Organizations do not need to adopt the entire framework.

Small teams can start with:

- Documentation standards
- Review process
- Architecture templates
- Project specifications

Additional components can be introduced as the organization grows.

---

## Can large enterprises use WBF?

Yes.

The framework has been designed to scale from small projects to enterprise environments.

Its modular structure allows organizations to adopt only the components that are relevant to their needs.

---

## Can existing projects adopt WBF?

Yes.

WBF is designed to support both:

- New projects
- Existing projects

Organizations may gradually introduce documentation standards, review processes, governance practices, and architecture templates without disrupting ongoing development.

---

# Framework

## Does WBF include coding standards?

The initial releases focus primarily on business analysis, architecture, documentation, governance, and engineering processes.

Language-specific coding standards may be introduced in future versions or maintained as separate companion guides.

---

## Does WBF include templates?

Yes.

The framework includes and will continue to expand its collection of:

- Requirement templates
- Architecture templates
- Review templates
- Project templates
- Documentation templates
- Checklists

---

## Does WBF prescribe a software architecture?

No.

WBF supports multiple architectural styles including:

- Layered Architecture
- Clean Architecture
- Hexagonal Architecture
- Microservices
- Event-Driven Systems
- Modular Monoliths

The framework focuses on documenting architecture rather than prescribing a single approach.

---

## Can WBF be customized?

Absolutely.

Organizations are encouraged to adapt templates, standards, and processes to suit their own engineering culture while preserving the core principles of the framework.

---

# Development Quality Platform (DQP)

## What is DQP?

The **WaysNX Development Quality Platform (DQP)** is the companion platform for WBF.

While WBF defines engineering practices, DQP automates and validates them.

Future capabilities include:

- Repository analysis
- Documentation validation
- Review automation
- Engineering dashboards
- Quality scoring
- Governance reporting
- Architecture compliance

---

## Is DQP required to use WBF?

No.

WBF is completely usable without DQP.

Organizations can adopt WBF independently and introduce DQP later if they wish to automate governance and quality assessment.
