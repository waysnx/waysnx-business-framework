# WBF-DOC-0112 – Business Process Reference Model

## Purpose

This specification defines the Business Process Reference Model (BPRM) within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It provides a standardized, technology-independent representation of enterprise business processes required to execute business capabilities and deliver organizational value.

The Business Process Reference Model establishes a common framework for identifying, organizing, analyzing, governing, improving, and automating business processes across the enterprise. It enables organizations to standardize operations, improve efficiency, support digital transformation, and align business execution with strategic objectives.

Unlike the Business Capability Reference Model, which defines **what** the enterprise does, the Business Process Reference Model defines **how** business capabilities are executed through repeatable business workflows.

This specification applies across all business units, operational functions, products, services, enterprise applications, and technology platforms.

# 2. Definitions

### Business Process

A structured sequence of activities performed to achieve a defined business objective and deliver value to customers or stakeholders.

---

### Process Owner

The individual accountable for the design, execution, performance, and continuous improvement of a business process.

---

### Process Map

A structured representation of business workflows, activities, decisions, roles, inputs, and outputs.

---

### Process Automation

The use of technology to execute, monitor, or optimize business processes with minimal manual intervention.

---

### Process KPI

A measurable indicator used to evaluate process effectiveness, efficiency, quality, compliance, or customer satisfaction.

# 11. Best Practices

- Define processes around business outcomes.
- Standardize enterprise workflows.
- Assign process ownership.
- Measure process performance.
- Automate repetitive activities.
- Minimize unnecessary approvals.
- Review processes regularly.
- Align processes with enterprise strategy.
- Continuously optimize business operations.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Confusing processes with capabilities
- Overly complex workflows
- Duplicate process definitions
- Undefined ownership
- Manual work where automation is feasible
- Technology-driven process design
- Missing KPIs
- Lack of governance
- Ignoring continuous improvement
