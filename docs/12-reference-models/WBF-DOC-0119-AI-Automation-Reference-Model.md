# WBF-DOC-0119 – AI & Automation Reference Model

## Purpose

This specification defines the AI & Automation Reference Model (AARM) within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes a standardized, technology-independent framework for applying Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Generative AI, Intelligent Automation, AI Agents, Decision Intelligence, and Robotic Process Automation (RPA) across the enterprise.

The AI & Automation Reference Model provides a common blueprint for designing, governing, deploying, monitoring, and continuously improving AI-powered capabilities while ensuring responsible AI, transparency, explainability, security, privacy, and compliance.

Rather than prescribing specific AI platforms, models, or vendors, this model defines logical AI capabilities and automation patterns that enable organizations to accelerate innovation, improve decision-making, automate repetitive work, and create intelligent digital enterprises.

This specification applies to enterprise applications, business processes, customer experiences, analytics platforms, cloud services, AI agents, knowledge systems, and automation platforms.

# 2. Definitions

### Artificial Intelligence

Computer systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, including learning, reasoning, perception, language understanding, and decision support.

---

### Generative AI

AI systems capable of generating new content such as text, images, code, audio, video, and structured business artifacts.

---

### AI Agent

An autonomous or semi-autonomous software entity capable of reasoning, planning, executing actions, interacting with users or systems, and accomplishing business objectives.

---

### Intelligent Automation

The combination of AI and automation technologies to automate complex business processes requiring decision-making or contextual understanding.

---

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

An AI architecture that enhances model responses by retrieving relevant enterprise knowledge during inference.

# 11. Best Practices

- Build AI solutions around measurable business outcomes.
- Establish enterprise knowledge management.
- Govern prompts, models, and AI agents.
- Keep humans involved in high-impact decisions.
- Monitor AI performance continuously.
- Build reusable AI services.
- Design explainable AI solutions.
- Protect sensitive information used by AI.
- Evaluate AI models regularly.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- AI without business value
- Uncontrolled AI adoption
- Shadow AI
- Ungoverned prompts
- Missing human oversight
- Training on untrusted data
- Ignoring AI bias
- Deploying AI without monitoring
- Vendor lock-in without abstraction
