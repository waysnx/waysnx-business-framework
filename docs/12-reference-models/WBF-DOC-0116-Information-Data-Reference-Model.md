# WBF-DOC-0116 – Information & Data Reference Model

## Purpose

This specification defines the Information & Data Reference Model (IDRM) within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes a standardized, technology-independent framework for organizing, governing, managing, sharing, protecting, and utilizing enterprise information and data assets.

The Information & Data Reference Model provides a common blueprint for business information architecture, data architecture, master data management, metadata management, analytics, reporting, data governance, and AI-ready information management.

The model distinguishes between business information (the meaning and context of information) and technical data (the physical representation and storage of information), ensuring enterprise-wide consistency and interoperability.

This specification applies to structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data managed across enterprise applications, cloud platforms, AI systems, business processes, integrations, and analytical environments.

# 2. Definitions

### Information

Business knowledge represented in a meaningful context that supports decision-making and business operations.

---

### Data

The digital representation of business information stored, processed, transmitted, or analyzed by enterprise systems.

---

### Master Data

Authoritative business entities shared across multiple enterprise applications.

Examples include:

- Customers
- Employees
- Products
- Vendors
- Organizations

---

### Metadata

Information describing the characteristics, structure, ownership, quality, and lifecycle of enterprise data assets.

---

### Data Governance

The organizational framework for managing data ownership, quality, security, compliance, and lifecycle.

- Maintain enterprise master data.

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Data silos
- Duplicate master data
- Poor data quality
- Undefined ownership
- Missing metadata
- Inconsistent terminology
- Weak governance
- Excessive data duplication
- Ignoring data lifecycle management
