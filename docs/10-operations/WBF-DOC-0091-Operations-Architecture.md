# WBF-DOC-0091 – Operations Architecture

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise Operations Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the architectural principles, operational capabilities, governance structures, lifecycle, and service delivery models required to operate enterprise systems efficiently, securely, reliably, and continuously.

Operations Architecture provides the foundation for managing enterprise applications, cloud platforms, infrastructure, business services, digital products, AI-enabled systems, and supporting operational processes throughout their lifecycle.

This specification is technology independent and serves as the foundational document for all operational disciplines within WBF.

# 1. Scope

This specification applies to:

- Enterprise Applications
- APIs
- Cloud Platforms
- Infrastructure
- Networks
- Databases
- Integration Platforms
- AI-enabled Systems
- Enterprise Services
- Digital Products

Operations Architecture governs the day-to-day operation, maintenance, support, and continuous improvement of enterprise technology services.

---

# 2. Definitions

### Operations Architecture

The enterprise architecture discipline responsible for designing, governing, and continuously improving the operational management of enterprise technology services.

### Operational Excellence

The ability to consistently deliver reliable, efficient, secure, and high-quality services that meet business objectives.

### Service Operations

Activities required to operate, monitor, maintain, support, and optimize enterprise services.

### Operational Readiness

The state in which systems, people, processes, and supporting assets are prepared for production operation.

### Operational Resilience

The capability of an organization to continue delivering services during disruptions while recovering rapidly from failures.

# 12. Best Practices

- Standardize operational procedures.
- Automate repetitive operational activities.
- Clearly define service ownership.
- Measure operational performance continuously.
- Design for resilience and recovery.
- Maintain operational documentation.
- Continuously review operational risks.
- Align operations with business priorities.
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement.

---

# 13. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Reactive operations
- Undefined service ownership
- Manual operational processes
- Poor operational visibility
- Inconsistent procedures
- Lack of operational documentation
- Ignoring capacity planning
- Siloed operational teams
- No continuous improvement strategy
