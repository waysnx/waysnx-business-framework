# WBF-DOC-0100 – Operations Governance

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise Operations Governance Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the governance model, organizational responsibilities, decision-making structures, operational policies, performance oversight, compliance mechanisms, and continuous improvement framework required to govern enterprise operations.

Operations Governance ensures that operational activities remain aligned with business strategy, enterprise architecture, regulatory obligations, security requirements, service commitments, and organizational objectives while promoting operational excellence and continuous improvement.

This specification is technology independent and applies to enterprise operations, cloud platforms, engineering organizations, business services, digital products, and supporting operational capabilities.

# 2. Definitions

### Operations Governance

The enterprise framework for directing, controlling, monitoring, and continually improving operational activities.

### Operational Policy

A formally approved rule or standard governing operational behavior.

### Operational Review

A structured assessment of operational performance, risks, compliance, and improvement opportunities.

### Operational Maturity

The capability of an organization to consistently deliver reliable, efficient, and governed operational services.

### Governance Board

A decision-making body responsible for operational oversight, strategic alignment, risk management, and continuous improvement.

# 11. Best Practices

- Establish clear operational ownership.
- Review KPIs regularly.
- Conduct governance meetings on a defined cadence.
- Audit operational compliance.
- Standardize operational policies.
- Encourage evidence-based decision making.
- Measure operational maturity.
- Integrate governance across business and technology.
- Continuously improve governance processes.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Governance without accountability
- Undefined operational ownership
- Policy without enforcement
- KPI overload
- Reactive governance
- Missing executive sponsorship
- Governance silos
- Ignoring operational audits
- Improvement initiatives without measurement
