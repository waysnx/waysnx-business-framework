# WBF-DOC-0099 – Operational Metrics & Measurement

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise Operational Metrics & Measurement Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the architectural principles, governance model, measurement framework, operational intelligence capabilities, and reporting mechanisms required to evaluate, optimize, and continuously improve enterprise operations.

Operational Metrics & Measurement provides the standardized approach for collecting, analyzing, reporting, and governing operational data across enterprise platforms, services, engineering teams, business operations, and technology ecosystems to support evidence-based decision making and continuous operational excellence.

This specification is technology independent and applies to enterprise applications, cloud platforms, infrastructure, AI-enabled systems, digital products, operational platforms, and supporting business services.

# 2. Definitions

### Operational Metric

A quantitative measurement that evaluates the performance, health, reliability, efficiency, or effectiveness of operational activities.

### Key Performance Indicator (KPI)

A strategic metric used to evaluate progress toward defined business or operational objectives.

### Service Level Indicator (SLI)

A quantitative measure of service performance used to evaluate operational reliability.

### Service Level Objective (SLO)

A defined target or objective for one or more service level indicators.

### Operational Dashboard

A consolidated visualization providing real-time and historical operational insights for engineering, operations, and business stakeholders.

# 11. Best Practices

- Define measurable operational objectives.
- Standardize enterprise KPIs.
- Automate metric collection.
- Build role-based dashboards.
- Continuously validate data quality.
- Measure outcomes rather than activity.
- Review operational trends regularly.
- Integrate metrics into governance.
- Continuously refine measurement models.

---

# 12. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Measuring everything without purpose
- Conflicting KPIs
- Manual metric collection
- Poor data quality
- Vanity metrics
- Ignoring trends
- Dashboards without ownership
- Metrics disconnected from business goals
- Reports that do not drive action
