# WBF-DOC-0092 – Service Management Architecture

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise Service Management Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the architectural principles, service lifecycle, governance model, operational capabilities, organizational responsibilities, and service delivery framework required to design, deliver, operate, support, and continuously improve enterprise technology and business services.

Service Management Architecture provides a structured approach to managing services throughout their lifecycle while ensuring alignment with business objectives, customer expectations, operational excellence, and enterprise architecture.

This specification is technology independent and serves as the enterprise architecture for service management regardless of implementation methodology or operational framework.

# 2. Definitions

### Service

A capability delivered to customers or business stakeholders that provides measurable value without exposing implementation complexity.

### Service Management

The coordinated activities required to design, deliver, operate, support, measure, and continually improve enterprise services.

### Service Owner

The individual accountable for the lifecycle, quality, performance, and continuous improvement of a service.

### Service Catalog

The centralized repository of enterprise services including ownership, capabilities, dependencies, SLAs, and lifecycle status.

### Service Level Agreement (SLA)

A documented commitment defining expected service performance, availability, responsiveness, and support obligations.

# 12. Best Practices

- Maintain a centralized service catalog.
- Clearly assign service ownership.
- Measure customer satisfaction.
- Define realistic SLAs.
- Standardize operational procedures.
- Automate repetitive service activities.
- Review services periodically.
- Continuously optimize customer experience.
- Align services with business priorities.

---

# 13. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Undefined service ownership
- Missing service catalog
- Technology-centric service definitions
- Unmeasured SLAs
- Reactive service delivery
- Poor customer communication
- Siloed operational teams
- Ignoring customer feedback
- No service improvement roadmap
