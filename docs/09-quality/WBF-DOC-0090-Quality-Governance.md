# WBF-DOC-0090 – Quality Governance

## Purpose

This specification defines the enterprise Quality Governance Architecture within the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF). It establishes the governance model, organizational structure, policies, decision-making processes, accountability, compliance mechanisms, strategic oversight, and continuous improvement practices required to manage quality consistently across the enterprise.

Quality Governance provides executive direction and organizational accountability for quality. It ensures that quality objectives are aligned with business strategy, engineering practices, operational excellence, customer expectations, regulatory obligations, and enterprise architecture.

This specification is technology independent and applies to enterprise applications, APIs, cloud platforms, AI-enabled systems, infrastructure, engineering organizations, and digital services.

# 2. Definitions

### Quality Governance

The enterprise governance framework that defines policies, organizational responsibilities, decision rights, oversight mechanisms, and accountability for achieving strategic quality objectives.

### Quality Policy

A formal statement defining enterprise quality expectations and commitments.

### Quality Portfolio

The collection of quality initiatives, investments, improvement programs, standards, and governance activities managed across the enterprise.

### Quality Review Board

A governance body responsible for reviewing enterprise quality performance, approving strategic initiatives, resolving escalated issues, and guiding continuous improvement.

### Quality Maturity

The organizational capability to consistently deliver high-quality products and services through governed, measurable, and continuously improving practices.

# 13. Best Practices

- Establish executive sponsorship for quality.
- Govern quality through measurable objectives.
- Create a cross-functional Quality Governance Board.
- Standardize enterprise quality policies.
- Review quality performance regularly.
- Benchmark organizational maturity.
- Encourage transparency and collaboration.
- Align investments with business priorities.
- Continuously evolve governance practices.

---

# 14. Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Treating quality as only a QA responsibility
- Lack of executive ownership
- Undefined quality policies
- Reactive governance
- Measuring activity instead of outcomes
- Siloed governance structures
- Ignoring customer feedback
- Inconsistent standards across teams
- No maturity improvement roadmap
