# CODE OF CONDUCT

> **Promoting a professional, respectful, and collaborative community for the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF).**

---

# Our Commitment

The **WaysNX Business Framework (WBF)** is built on the belief that great engineering is the result of collaboration, continuous learning, and mutual respect.

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, and professional environment where contributors from all backgrounds can participate, share ideas, and improve the framework together.

Every participant deserves to be treated with dignity, courtesy, and professionalism.

---

# Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the expected standards of behavior for everyone participating in the WaysNX Business Framework community.

Its objectives are to:

- Promote respectful collaboration
- Encourage constructive technical discussions
- Maintain a welcoming environment
- Support knowledge sharing
- Protect contributors from harassment and discrimination
- Preserve the integrity of the framework
- Build a sustainable engineering community

This Code of Conduct applies to all project interactions, including discussions, issues, pull requests, documentation reviews, and community communications.

---

# Our Values

The WaysNX Business Framework is guided by the following principles.

## Professionalism

We conduct ourselves professionally in every interaction.

Respectful communication builds stronger engineering communities and better technical outcomes.

---

## Respect

Treat everyone with respect regardless of:

- Experience level
- Technical background
- Organization
- Nationality
- Gender
- Age
- Education
- Language
- Culture

Respectful disagreement is encouraged.

Disrespect is not.

---

## Collaboration

Engineering is a collaborative discipline.

We encourage:

- Knowledge sharing
- Constructive reviews
- Open discussions
- Mentoring
- Continuous learning
- Teamwork

The best solutions often emerge from diverse perspectives.

---

## Technical Excellence

Technical discussions should be guided by:

- Evidence
- Experience
- Engineering principles
- Architecture
- Maintainability
- Quality

Decisions should focus on improving the framework rather than promoting personal preferences.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Mistakes are opportunities to learn.

Constructive feedback helps improve both the framework and the community.

---

# Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful
- Be constructive
- Listen to others
- Welcome new contributors
- Provide helpful feedback
- Share knowledge openly
- Support collaborative problem solving
- Respect different viewpoints
- Focus discussions on technical topics
- Follow project guidelines
- Accept review feedback professionally
- Encourage continuous improvement

Professional behavior strengthens the community.

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable.

## Harassment

Including but not limited to:

- Personal attacks
- Threats
- Intimidation
- Bullying
- Offensive comments
- Discriminatory language
- Repeated unwanted contact

---

## Disruptive Behavior

Examples include:

- Trolling
- Deliberately provoking arguments
- Repeated off-topic discussions
- Intentional disruption of reviews
- Spamming
- Misrepresentation of facts

---

## Unprofessional Conduct

Examples include:

- Hostile communication
- Public humiliation
- Insults
- Personal criticism unrelated to technical work
- Dismissive or abusive responses

Critique ideas—not people.

---

## Misuse of the Framework

Examples include:

- Deliberate misinformation
- Plagiarism
- Submitting malicious content
- Falsifying contributions
- Knowingly introducing misleading documentation
- Attempting to damage the integrity of the project

---

# Technical Discussions

Healthy technical debate is encouraged.

Participants should:

- Support opinions with evidence
- Explain architectural reasoning
- Respect alternative approaches
- Avoid emotional arguments
- Focus on long-term maintainability
- Separate facts from opinions

Technical disagreements should improve the framework—not divide the community.

---

# Review Etiquette

When reviewing contributions:

- Be respectful
- Be objective
- Explain suggestions clearly
- Focus on improving the contribution
- Avoid dismissive comments
- Recognize good work
- Provide actionable feedback

Reviews should educate as well as evaluate.

---

# Communication Guidelines

When communicating within the project:

- Assume positive intent
- Ask questions before making assumptions
- Keep discussions constructive
- Remain patient
- Use inclusive language
- Avoid sarcasm that may be misunderstood
- Respect cultural differences

Clear communication reduces misunderstandings.

---

# Reporting Concerns

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

- Report the issue privately to the project maintainers.
- Provide relevant details and supporting information.
- Avoid public escalation whenever possible.
- Allow maintainers the opportunity to investigate fairly.

All reports will be reviewed respectfully and, where appropriate, confidentially.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity of the issue, actions may include:

- Private guidance
- Informal warning
- Formal warning
- Temporary restriction from participation
- Removal of inappropriate content
- Suspension of contributor privileges
- Permanent removal from the project community

Enforcement decisions will be made fairly and proportionately.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Documentation reviews
- Project discussions
- Community forums
- Official project communication channels
- Events organized under the WaysNX Business Framework

Participants are expected to uphold these standards whenever representing the project.

---

# A Positive Community

A healthy engineering community is built through:

- Curiosity
- Respect
- Collaboration
- Transparency
- Professionalism
- Continuous learning

Every contributor has the opportunity to help create a welcoming and productive environment.
