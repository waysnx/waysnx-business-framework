# WBF-DOC-0001
# WaysNX Business Framework (WBF) Manifesto

## 1. Purpose

The WaysNX Business Framework (WBF) exists to provide a structured, implementation-independent approach to designing, developing and maintaining business software.

Modern software projects often become tightly coupled to programming languages, frameworks, libraries or cloud platforms. As systems evolve, business logic becomes scattered across applications, making software difficult to understand, maintain and reuse.

WBF addresses this problem by placing business concepts at the center of software engineering. The framework defines business behavior through specifications that remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

## 2. The Problem

Business applications frequently suffer from one or more of the following challenges:

- Business rules are embedded directly in application code.
- Different teams implement the same business process differently.
- Technology choices influence business design.
- Documentation becomes outdated or incomplete.
- Business knowledge is difficult to transfer between teams.
- Applications become expensive to maintain over time.

These challenges reduce software quality, increase maintenance costs and make organizations dependent on specific technologies or implementations.

---

## 3. Our Beliefs

The WaysNX Business Framework is built upon the following beliefs.

### Business Before Technology

Business requirements should define software behavior. Technology should implement those requirements rather than shape them.

### Specifications Before Implementation

A clear specification reduces ambiguity, improves communication and provides a stable foundation for implementation.

### Consistency Builds Trust

Consistent terminology, architecture and behavior improve developer productivity and reduce implementation errors.

### Quality Is a Feature

Reliability, maintainability, security, performance and documentation are essential characteristics of quality software, not optional enhancements.

### Simplicity Enables Adoption

Complex frameworks discourage adoption. WBF favors simple, well-defined concepts that can be understood and implemented consistently.

---

## 4. Vision

Our vision is to establish a framework that enables organizations to define business systems independently of implementation technologies.

A WBF specification should be capable of being implemented using different programming languages, frameworks or platforms while preserving identical business behavior.

The specification becomes the long-term asset; implementations become replaceable.

---

## 5. Design Philosophy

WBF is guided by the following philosophy.

- Business concepts are the primary abstraction.
- Specifications define expected behavior.
- Implementations conform to specifications.
- Standards encourage interoperability.
- Simplicity is preferred over unnecessary complexity.
- Explicit behavior is preferred over implicit behavior.
- Long-term maintainability is valued over short-term convenience.

---

## 6. What WBF Is

The WaysNX Business Framework is:

- A specification-driven engineering framework.
- A standard for describing business behavior.
- A foundation for building consistent business applications.
- A framework that encourages technology independence.
- A reference for implementing interoperable business systems.
- A platform for creating reusable business capabilities.

---

## 7. What WBF Is Not

The WaysNX Business Framework is not:

- A programming language.
- A web framework.
- A UI framework.
- A replacement for existing development platforms.
- A low-code platform.
- A business process management suite.
- A product tied to a single vendor or technology stack.

---

## 8. Success Criteria

The framework is considered successful when:

- Specifications remain stable over time.
- Multiple implementations conform to the same specification.
- Business behavior remains consistent across technologies.
- Developers can understand business functionality without reading source code.
- Organizations can evolve implementation technologies without redefining business logic.

---

## 9. Commitment

Every WBF specification should contribute to a framework that is:

- Clear
- Consistent
- Practical
- Technology independent
- Testable
- Maintainable
- Extensible

These characteristics should guide every architectural and engineering decision within the WaysNX Business Framework.

---

## 10. Guiding Statement

The WaysNX Business Framework exists to separate business intent from technical implementation.

Specifications define the business.

Implementations realize the specification.

Technology evolves.

Business knowledge endures.
