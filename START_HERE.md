# START HERE

> **Your guide to exploring and adopting the WaysNX Business Framework (WBF).**

Welcome to the **WaysNX Business Framework (WBF)**.

Whether you're an executive evaluating the framework, an architect reviewing its principles, a developer exploring its standards, or a contributor looking to get involved, this guide will help you navigate the repository efficiently.

Unlike traditional software projects, WBF is not a framework that you install—it is a framework that you **understand, adopt, and apply**. The repository contains architecture blueprints, engineering standards, specifications, governance models, templates, schemas, and reference guidance that together define a structured approach to building modern enterprise software.

This document serves as the primary navigation guide for the framework and will help you:

- Understand the purpose and scope of WBF
- Choose the reading path that best matches your role
- Navigate the repository effectively
- Identify the documents most relevant to your objectives
- Plan your adoption journey
- Discover opportunities to contribute to the framework

## Repository Design Principles

The repository is organized according to a few guiding principles:

- **Separation of Responsibilities** – Documentation, specifications, templates, schemas, and examples are maintained independently while remaining closely connected.
- **Scalability** – The structure is designed to accommodate future domains, standards, and ecosystem projects without requiring major reorganization.
- **Discoverability** – Information is organized so that users can locate relevant content quickly based on their role and objectives.
- **Consistency** – Similar content follows common naming conventions, layouts, and documentation standards.
- **Extensibility** – New specifications, templates, and guidance can be added with minimal impact on the existing repository structure.
